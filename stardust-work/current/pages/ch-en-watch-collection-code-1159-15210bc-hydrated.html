Code 11.59 by Audemars Piguet
Selfwinding

Ref. 15210BC.OO.A002KB.01
30 900 CHF
Plan an Appointment

The Code 11.59 by Audemars Piguet Selfwinding presents an 18-carat white gold case complemented by a smoked blue sunburst lacquered dial and a black rubber-coated strap.

Case

18-carat white gold case, bezel, lugs and caseback, double glareproofed sapphire crystal and caseback.

  • Size 41 mm
  • Thickness 10.7 mm
  • Water Resistance 30 m

Dial

Smoked blue lacquered dial with sunburst pattern, white gold applied hour-markers and hands, black lacquered inner bezel.

Bracelet

Black rubber-coated strap with 18-carat white gold pin buckle.
