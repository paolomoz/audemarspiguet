Caring for you watch

Can I swim with my watch?

Yes, if your watch is water-resistant. Make sure to comform to the depth indicated in the technical specifications and always check that the crown and push-pieces are fully screwed down before immersing your watch in water.

We encourage you to check up your watch water-resistance at least on a yearly basis. Such check-up is complimentary in all our Audemars Piguet boutiques and not warrantied.

Avoid contact with water for non-water-resistant watches and watches fitted with leather straps.

My watch is running fast. Why?

If your watch is running fast, it may have been exposed to a magnetic field. If necessary, a simple demagnetisation process can be performed at an Audemars Piguet boutique, service centre or authorised retailer. Otherwise, your watch might need a complete maintenance service.

Can a shock disturb my watch?

As the result of a shock, the oil inside the movement can disperse, the pivots run dry and clearances in the wheel train may alter, ultimately making the movement unstable and in need of a maintenance service.

Impacts could cose an exclusion of the warranty coverage. For more information, please refer to our Customer Service General Terms & Conditions.

What is the difference between a Certificate of Authenticity and an Extract from the Archives?

The Certificate of Authenticity is made at our Manufacture in Switzerland after the watch is evaluated there. This document contains information about the watch and actual pictures.

The Extract from the Archives (or Extrait de Registre) does not authenticate a watch. It only certifies that the case and movement numbers appear in our records. Please note that only a Certificate of Authenticity guarantees the authenticity of the watch.

AP Coverage Service

What is the scope of the AP Coverage Service?

The AP Coverage Service is a 2-year complimentary worldwide service offered under certain conditions by Audemars Piguet to end-customers. 

It is available for all Audemars Piguet watches initially acquired in 2024, 2025 and 2026, in our Audemars Piguet boutiques, AP Houses or via official retailers.

It protects AP Watches from the risk of burglary and robbery as well as functional damage, due to external factors occurring suddenly and unexpectedly, by offering the possibility, free of charge, to repair, refund or replace the watch, at the sole discretion of Audemars Piguet.

What is excluded from the AP Coverage Service?

The following cases are excluded from the AP Coverage Service: 

Loss, simple theft (only burglary and robbery will be considered), normal wear and tear, aesthetic damage, damage resulting from air humidity or temperature influences, damage already covered by the AP International Sales Warranty, watches that have undergone third-party intervention or third-party modification, damage to a strap, voluntary damage, damage when the watch is in the possession of a third party, damage in connection with war and rebellion, confiscation by authorities, damage in relation to earthquakes, nuclear energy, radioactivity, chemical and biological weapons, damage in relation to cyber incidents, theft committed by persons living in the same household and fraud.

What are the prerequisites to activate the AP Coverage Service?

The granting of the AP Coverage Service by Audemars Piguet is neither automatic nor unconditional. Prior the occurrence of either the burglary or robbery as well as the damage, you shall activate the service in your name, for each eligible watch:

1. Log in / Create an AP Account on our website. If you bought your watch in an Audemars Piguet boutique or an AP House, we invite you to use the email address provided at the time of the purchase – your watch should automatically appear in your account.

2. Register your watch. If your watch does not appear on your AP Account, you can register it online. 

3. Open your watch details and click on the link “Activate AP Coverage Service" to complete the form. 

4. Audemars Piguet will review your request and get back to you to confirm the activation of your watch's AP Coverage Service.

Audemars Piguet reserves its right, at its sole discretion, to modify the prerequisites of the AP Coverage Service's activation at any time and in any manner.

What is the duration of the AP Coverage Service?

The service is available for all AP watches initially acquired in 2024, 2025 and 2026, in Audemars Piguet’s boutiques, AP Houses or via official retailers. All AP watches initially acquired in 2024, 2025 and 2026 are covered for a period of 2 years from the date of the initial purchase.

You may still declare the burglary, robbery or damage up to 5 days after the end of the protection period as long as the service has been successfully activated within the protection period and the burglary, robbery or damage occurred during said period.

We invite you to visit your AP account to check the validity end-date of your watch's AP Coverage Service.

Is any AP watch, regardless of its value, eligible for the AP Coverage Service?

Eligible watches for AP Coverage, and for which AP Coverage has been correctly activated, will be refunded or replaced at Audemars Piguet’s discretion, exclusively up to CHF 500,000 (five hundred thousand Swiss francs), regardless of the watch’s value(1).

(1) the value refers to the actual price paid at the time of the original purchase

What are the main advantages of the AP Coverage Service compared to an individual insurance covering theft and damage?

The AP Coverage Service is not an insurance. It is a discretionary service offered by Audemars Piguet free of charge. Therefore, no premium or deductible are payable by end-customers.

Compared to other similar services, our AP Coverage Service goes beyond the coverage offered by all-risk insurance on the market. Moreover, a service of this extent is difficult, if not impossible, to obtain in certain countries under similar conditions from insurance providers.

The AP Coverage Service may be granted by means of facilitated procedures, via an accelerated announcement and processing procedure and dedicated contact persons. In addition, the qualifying conditions are simplified.

In the end, the watch announced as stolen will be recorded as such in our files, which will allow Audemars Piguet to identify it in the event of a return to our network.

About Spoofing

What is spoofing?

Spoofing is a cybercrime tactic where attackers disguise their identity to appear as a trusted entity, such as Audemars Piguet. They use fraudulent emails, phone calls, or messages to deceive individuals into sharing sensitive information, making payments to illegitimate accounts, or engaging in fraudulent activities.

How can I recognize a spoofing attempt?

Be cautious of:

- Unexpected or unusual communications claiming to be from Audemars Piguet.
- Requests for personal, financial, or login information.
- Urgent demands for payment or other immediate actions.
- Emails with suspicious links or attachments.

How can I verify if a message is genuinely from Audemars Piguet?

- Check the sender's email address carefully – spoofed emails often have slight variations from official addresses.
- Look for grammatical errors or inconsistencies in tone.
- Contact your usual Audemars Piguet representative or reach out to us via our official contact details listed on our website.
- Forward the message to our Client Care Cells

What should I do if I receive a suspicious email, call, or message?

- Do not click on any links or open attachments.
- Do not provide any personal or financial information.
- Verify the request by contacting Audemars Piguet directly through our official channels.
- Forward the message to our Client Care Cells

Will Audemars Piguet ever ask me for sensitive information via email or phone?

No, Audemars Piguet will never request confidential information, passwords, or payments via email, phone, or text messages. If you receive such a request, it is likely fraudulent.

What should I do if I have already responded to a spoofing attempt?

- Immediately stop any further communication with the sender.
- If you have shared personal or financial information, contact your bank and relevant authorities.
- Inform Audemars Piguet’s client support team so we can provide further guidance.
- Forward the message to our Client Care Cells

Who can I contact if I suspect a spoofing attempt?

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from Audemars Piguet, contact us directly through our official phone number or email listed on our website. Your usual point of contact and our client support team are available to assist you. Your security is our priority, and we appreciate your vigilance in helping maintain a safe and trusted community.
