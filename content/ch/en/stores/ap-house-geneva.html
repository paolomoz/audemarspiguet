quote

Step into our AP House, an innovative and intimate concept that embodies Audemars Piguet’s forward-thinking approach to retail. Located at Quai du Mont-Blanc 5, this temporary AP House is designed for discovery, conversation and shared experiences. A complement to our Geneva boutique, it is a welcoming space where refinement, comfort and hospitality come together to create memorable moments. Join a community where genuine connections and a shared passion for fine watchmaking are at the heart of the experience.

About this
AP House

Audemars Piguet’s forward-thinking approach to retail. Whether you wish to discover our watches, explore AP’s universe, or simply spend time with us, our doors are always open. Designed as a timeless space for lovers of the brand, the AP House was created in collaboration with some of the Manufacture’s most loyal clients, imagining how brand founders Jules Louis Audemars and Edward Auguste Piguet would deal with their clients if they were living in the 21st century, travelling the world and sharing their passion for beautiful watches. The result is a space where the comfort of home and the warmth of friendship take priority.
