Discover the Royal Oak, an icon of watchmaking. Seated around an experiential table, participants delve into the story of this emblematic timepiece through a curated selection of heritage watches and rare archival documents.

Each detail, evolution and source of inspiration is revealed, offering deeper insight into what defines the Royal Oak. The session concludes with an educational activity inspired by its universe, allowing each participant to take home a personal memento of the experience.

MASTERCLASSES - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When do I need to pay my registration fee?

Once you have registered for a masterclass, you will receive an email from the boutique where it is taking place. The email will contain a secure payment link and details of how to pay.

How do I make a payment?

Once you have registered, you will receive an email from the boutique where the masterclass is taking place with a secure payment link.

Can I change or cancel my enrolment in a masterclass?

The buyer can amend or cancel their reservation up to three (3) days before the date of the masterclass indicated on the reservation confirmation and will then receive a full refund. For more detail, please refer to the General Conditions of Sale available here.

Do I need to accompany my child to the "Little Watchmakers" masterclass?

Children registered for the "Little Watchmakers" masterclass need to be accompanied by an adult during the event.

Do I need to create an account to register for a masterclass?

Yes, it is necessary to create an account to enroll in a masterclass.

Is there a minimum age requirement to participate in a masterclass?

Yes, the masterclasses are open to anyone aged 16 and over. The "Little Watchmakers" masterclass is an exception, however, as it is aimed at children aged 8 to 12 who are accompanied by an adult.

May I bring a guest to the masterclass?

No. Anyone who wishes to attend a masterclass must register. ONLY THE PEOPLE NAMED ON THE RESERVATION MAY ATTEND.

How do I cancel my registration and what are the cancellation terms?

YOU MAY CANCEL YOUR REGISTRATION IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE TERMS SET OUT IN CLAUSE 5 OF OUR GENERAL CONDITIONS OF SALE (CANCELLATION POLICY). Please refer to our general terms and conditions for more information.

Are the masterclasses accessible to people with reduced mobility?

Unfortunately, we are unable to welcome wheelchairs users to our masterclasses at this time due to practical reasons.

Can I take pictures during a masterclass?

Yes, you are allowed to take pictures during a masterclass.

Can I participate in a masterclass without registering beforehand?

No, anyone who wishes to attend a masterclass must register on our website beforehand. You can enroll up to three (3) months before the date of the masterclass via this link.

How can I register for a masterclass?

You can register for one of our masterclasses on our website, using this link.

In which languages are the masterclasses available?

What happens if Audemars Piguet cancels the masterclass?

In the unlikely event that the organisor has to cancel the masterclass, the fee will be fully refunded. The refund will be credited to the same debit/credit card or bank account used for the initial purchase. No other compensation will be offered. Masterclasses are non-smoking areas and pets are not permitted. Eating and drinking is also strictly forbidden in the space reserved for the masterclass.
